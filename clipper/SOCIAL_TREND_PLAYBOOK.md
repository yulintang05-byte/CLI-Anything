# Social Trend & Theme-Page Playbook (research snapshot, 2026-11-07)

Source quality note: findings come from web search of public blogs/trackers. Many are vendor/course-seller content; treat numbers as directional. No account credentials or platform APIs were available, so nothing was posted or changed on any account.

## TikTok (snapshot)
- Tokchart fastest-growing worldwide hashtags (1 Nov 2026): #musteatinmycity, #xuhuong1111, #komanche. Hashtags go stale daily and vary by niche, so verify live.
- Live sources to check each run: TikTok Creative Center (Trend Discovery: hashtags, songs, creators; filter songs "Approved for Business Use" for commercial accounts), tiktok.com/trends, https://tokchart.com/hashtags, https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/tiktok-trends (weekly sounds list).
- Rule: apply a trending sound to your own niche rather than generic lip-sync.

## YouTube Shorts
- YouTube removed its public Trending page (2025); discover via Shorts search/autocomplete, competitor channels, and cross-platform signals.
- Recurring formats: "wait for it" reveals, "I tested X viral hacks", horror/history/true-crime mini-stories, skits, unpopular-opinion/explainers.
- Retention and replays (loopable endings) drive distribution.

## Converting theme pages
1. Niche first: finance/entrepreneurship and AI/tech-business reported as top earners; wrong niche is the most common failure.
2. Funnel: strangers (viral content) -> nurture (value posts) -> convert (link-in-bio, email/subscribers, product).
3. Monetization ladder: affiliate + discount codes (once warm traffic exists) -> shoutouts (~10K-50K followers, 2-5% engagement) -> own product -> optional page sale.
4. TikTok captions can't carry clickable links; use link-in-bio. Pick offers before scaling volume.
5. Check each platform's current policy on affiliate disclosure and shoutouts directly.

## Per-account optimization checklist
- Bio states niche + single CTA; one link-in-bio page.
- Pin 3 best converting posts; consistent visual theme.
- Post 1-3x/day; reuse top performer formats; log hook, sound, hashtags, 3s/full retention.
- Weekly: pull trends from sources above, pick 3 sounds + 5 niche hashtags, drop anything below median performance.
