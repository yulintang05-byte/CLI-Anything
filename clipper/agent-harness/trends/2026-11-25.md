## 5. Theme pages that convert (playbook)

1. **Niche first, then monetization.** Pick a niche with a buyer intent product (finance/budgeting, AI tools, fitness, home/decor, gifts). Niche and offer matter more than follower count.
2. **Funnel:** viral reach content → value/series content → offer (affiliate, TikTok Shop, digital product, email list). Keep a 70/20/10 mix.
3. **Monetization stack, easiest first:** affiliate / TikTok Shop → shoutouts/brand deals (example pricing for a ~100k page: $20–50 per story, $50–150 per post) → own digital product → sell the page.
4. **Stay compliant.** Make original or licensed content, or curate with credit. Unoriginal reposts get no reach and lose Creator Rewards and Reels bonus eligibility. Disclose affiliate links with the #ad tag or the paid-partnership label.
5. **Learning resources** (vendor blogs, so verify any numbers): stan.store/blog/faceless-account, earnifyhub.com (theme page income 2026), TikTok Creator Academy, TikTok Creative Center, YouTube Creator Academy.
