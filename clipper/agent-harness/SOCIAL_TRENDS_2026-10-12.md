## Converting theme pages (recurring advice, income claims unverified)
1. Pick a niche with proven demand that is not too broad.
2. Optimize the profile: recognizable photo, keyword name, bio with one clear CTA.
3. Recreate winning hooks with your own footage rather than reposting.
4. Funnel: link-in-bio landing page with an email capture, then affiliate or own products. Email is the platform-independent asset.
5. Cross-post to TikTok and Shorts so no single platform ban kills the business.
6. Monetization: affiliate, sponsored posts, shoutouts, digital products, selling the page.
Sources: [Faceless Freedom](https://faceless-freedom.beehiiv.com/p/make-money-with-instagram), [Digital Creator](https://creator-daniel.beehiiv.com/p/heres-grow-monetize-theme-page)
