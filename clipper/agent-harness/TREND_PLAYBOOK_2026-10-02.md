## Converting theme page recipe
1. Pick a high-CPM faceless niche (finance literacy, AI productivity, stoic mindset, luxury real estate).
2. Bio = one promise + one link (digital product $17-$47, or SaaS affiliate with recurring commission).
3. Post 1-3x/day on trending sounds; cross-post TikTok / Reels / Shorts (strip watermarks, use original or licensed clips; credit sources).
4. Hook in first 1-2s, 3-5 niche hashtags + 1-2 trend tags, CTA "link in bio" / comment keyword.
5. Monetize in order: affiliate/digital product -> sponsorships (~50k engaged followers, $250-750/Reel) -> platform programs.
6. Review analytics weekly; double down on top 10% of posts, drop the rest.
