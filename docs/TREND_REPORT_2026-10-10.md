## Converting theme pages
1. Pick one narrow niche; on-topic handle; Creator account.
2. Original or meaningfully transformed content only — Instagram suppresses reposting accounts.
3. Funnel: reach content -> CTA (bio link or "comment KEYWORD") -> DM automation / landing page / email list -> paid offer (digital product, affiliate, sponsored posts).
4. Monetization typically starts at 10k-50k followers; TikTok native payouts are minimal (~$0.02-0.05 per 1k views), so treat TikTok as top of funnel.
5. Cross-post: TikTok -> Reels -> Shorts (remove watermarks, native edit).

## Account optimization checklist (to be applied by account owner)
- Bio: niche keyword + single clear CTA + link.
- Post 1-3x/day on trend sounds + seasonal hashtags (3-5 per post, mix broad/niche).
- Track retention and shares; double down on top 10% videos.
