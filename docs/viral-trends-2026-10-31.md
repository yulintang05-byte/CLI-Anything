## YouTube Shorts
- Retention is the key metric; "wait for it" reveal hooks; story formats (horror, history, true crime recaps, mini-docs); music remix/lyric/live clips; repeatable series ("one tip", "best moment of the day"); visible-stakes challenges; gaming.
- Sources: https://air.io/en/youtube-hacks/13-types-of-shorts-that-always-go-viral-with-examples , https://kineclip.com/blog/short-form-video-trends-2026/ , https://blog.mean.ceo/?p=10944

## Converting theme pages
1. One tight niche; consistent visual identity and posting cadence.
2. Cross-post one asset to Reels/TikTok/Shorts; native, original edits (avoid reposting watermarks).
3. Bio = who it's for + one CTA + multi-link/landing page; capture emails.
4. Monetize in order: affiliate -> low-ticket digital product -> own offer -> sponsored posts.
5. Avoid follow/unfollow and botting (ban risk). Treat income claims in courses as unverified.
6. Track per post: hook retention, shares/saves, link clicks, email signups; double down on top 20%.

## Account optimization checklist
- Bio/CTA/link audit; pinned posts = best converting content.
- 3-5 hashtags max, mix 1 trending + 2 niche; trending sound only if it fits the niche.
- Post 1-3x/day on the best-performing format; review analytics weekly.
