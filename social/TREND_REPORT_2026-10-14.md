## Theme-page playbook (converting)
1. Pick one niche; audit top accounts' content, naming, monetization.
2. Searchable name + specific bio + single CTA to link-in-bio landing page.
3. Curate/remix proven (100k+ view) formats; schedule consistently; cross-post TikTok/Shorts/Reels.
4. Convert via email lead magnet -> low-priced ($5–$25) digital product; affiliate links go behind a landing page (platform link restrictions).
5. Engage comments/DMs, collab with niche pages, track Insights, double down on top posts. Respect platform rules on reposting/credit and branded content.
