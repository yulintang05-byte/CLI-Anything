## Converting theme pages
1. One narrow niche with clear buyers (finance, AI productivity, stoicism, luxury real estate); broad pages don't monetize.
2. Original or meaningfully transformed content; Instagram limits reach for repost-heavy accounts.
3. Monetize early: affiliate/SaaS recurring commissions (~30%), $17-$47 digital products, then brand deals.
4. Thresholds: TikTok 10k followers + 100k views/30d; Instagram subscriptions 10k + professional account.
5. Sources: https://foxy.ai/academy/how-to-start-an-instagram-theme-page-2026 · https://www.remogrid.com/blog/online-income/how-to-make-money-with-faceless-instagram-theme-pages-2026-step-by-step

## Per-account checklist (apply manually)
- Pick 2-3 trending sounds matching the account niche; post within days of the trend (trends decay fast)
- 3-5 niche hashtags + 1-2 trend hashtags; hook in first 2s
- Bio: single clear promise + link-in-bio to the offer
- Pin 3 best/most converting posts; track saves/shares over likes
