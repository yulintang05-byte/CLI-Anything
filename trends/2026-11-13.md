## 3. Account optimization checklist (apply to every account)
1. Bio = who it's for + what they get + CTA → one link (Linktree/Beacons/Stan) to the offer or email list.
2. One niche per account; first 3 pinned posts = best performers / "start here".
3. Hook in first 1–2 s with on-screen text; captions on every video; 7–30 s for Shorts/TikTok.
4. Post 1–3x/day; cross-post every clip to TikTok, Shorts, Reels (strip watermarks).
5. Ride trending sound **within 24–72 h**; use the in-app sound, not re-uploaded audio.
6. Weekly: kill formats below median views, double down on top 20%.

## 4. Converting theme pages — playbook
- **Model:** attract (trend-led posts) → nurture (value posts, saves/shares) → convert (link in bio: email list, digital product, affiliate).
