## Converting theme-page playbook
1. One focused niche with proven demand; study top accounts' naming, content and monetization.
2. Keyword-rich name, recognizable avatar, bio with one clear CTA.
3. Recreate trending formats in your own style (prefer original/licensed footage over straight reposts — copyright and reach risk).
4. Cross-post to TikTok, Reels, Shorts; schedule ahead; swap shoutouts with similar pages.
5. Monetize via affiliate/own product through a link-in-bio bridge page with email capture (Instagram may block raw affiliate links).
6. Match offers to audience problems; check platform rules on affiliate/sponsored disclosure.
Sources: [Faceless Freedom](https://faceless-freedom.beehiiv.com/p/make-money-with-instagram), [Wifi Wealthy](https://wifiwealthy.beehiiv.com/p/make-passive-income-instagram-theme-pages), [Udemy course](https://www.udemy.com/course/instagram-marketing-how-to-grow-a-theme-page-for-business/)
