# Converting Theme Pages: Playbook

A theme page is a faceless account built around one tight niche (quotes, memes, gaming clips, luxury, fitness, etc.). It **converts** when followers take an action that makes money: they click the bio link, buy, join the email list, or book a shoutout.

> Sources are mostly creator/vendor guides (Stan, Influish, Faceless.my, Pixteller and others). The income figures they quote are unverified anecdotes, so treat the method as the useful part, not the numbers.

## 1. Pick the niche (do this before posting)
- One page = one audience = one product category. **Tight niches convert better** (e.g. "stoic quotes for men 18–30" beats "motivation").
- Validate the niche: find 5 pages in it with more than 100K followers whose posts get more than 50K views. If you can't, there's no proven demand.
- Map the money first: is there an affiliate product, digital product, or TikTok Shop item this audience already buys?

## 2. Optimize the profile (apply to ALL accounts)
- **Handle:** short, niche-keyword, no numbers or underscores spam (e.g. `@dailystoicmind`).
- **Name field:** contains the search keyword (Instagram and TikTok index it). Example: "Stoic Quotes | Discipline".
- **Bio formula:** *who it's for → what they get → CTA*. Example: "Daily discipline for men who want more 📈 / Free 30-day guide ⬇️".
- **One link** (Stan / Beacons / Linktree), with **the first button = the main offer** (a free lead magnet beats a paid product for cold traffic).
- Consistent avatar/logo across TikTok, YT Shorts, and IG so people recognize the brand.
- TikTok: switch to a Business account only if you need analytics/links. Business accounts lose most copyrighted trending sounds (use the Commercial Music Library).
- Pin 3 posts: best performer, "start here"/series Part 1, and a direct offer post.

## 3. Content system
- **Volume:** 1–3 posts/day per platform for the first 30 days, at fixed times.
- **Mix (70/20/10):** 70% reach content (trend sounds/formats from the daily brief), 20% value/series content (builds followers), 10% direct CTA content.
- **Hook in under 1 s:** text overlay plus motion; no logo intro.
- **Series** ("Part 1/5") drive profile visits, and profile visits drive link clicks.
- Repurpose: TikTok → Shorts → Reels with the watermark removed. Use `clipper` presets `tiktok`, `youtube_short`, `instagram`.
- Use the trending audio *at low volume under original text/voice*, which keeps the trend boost without losing the theme.

## 4. Conversion mechanics
1. **Comment-trigger CTA:** "Comment GUIDE and I'll send it" (use ManyChat/auto-DM on IG). This boosts engagement and captures leads.
2. **Lead magnet → email list:** a free PDF/checklist, then an email sequence sells the paid product. You own the list; the platform can ban the page.
3. **Revenue streams, in order of reliability:**
   - Own digital products (guides, templates, presets, Notion packs): highest margin.
   - Affiliate offers that fit the niche, plus TikTok Shop affiliate (big right now with #tiktokshopblackfriday).
   - Paid shoutouts/promos to other pages and brands (rate ≈ followers ÷ 1,000 × $1–5 to start).
   - Platform payouts (TikTok Creator Rewards needs videos longer than 1 min; YouTube Partner Program). These are low and should be treated as a bonus.
4. Track: views → profile visits → link clicks → leads → sales. Fix the weakest step first.

## 5. Things that get pages killed
- Reposting other creators' videos without transformation or credit (copyright strikes).
- Engagement pods or bought followers (Instagram flags them as inauthentic).
- Unrelated trending hashtags; undisclosed AI visuals (label them); unlicensed music on Business accounts.

## 6. 30-day launch checklist
- [ ] Day 1: niche picked, money mapped, profile optimized on all 3 platforms
- [ ] Day 1: lead magnet + link page live
- [ ] Days 1–30: 2 posts/day using the daily trend brief (`growth/trends/`)
- [ ] Day 7: double down on the top 2 formats and drop the bottom 50%
- [ ] Day 14: start the comment-trigger CTA plus a series
- [ ] Day 30: review the funnel numbers, launch a paid product or affiliate push
